# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Sushi to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
background, identity, experience or ability. Many people who use it are
looking for work, which can be stressful, so be patient and kind.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect people's privacy: never share someone's CV, contact details or
  application history.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, code or issues that break this code, and may
ban people from the project temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or
issue, or contact the maintainers of the
[getsushi](https://github.com/getsushi) organisation privately. Reports are
handled confidentially.
